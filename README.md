### Hi, I'm Jack

I build and review AI video tooling. Notes on what I actually use:

- [Rea Video](https://reavideo.pro/) — breaks a video down shot by shot and returns a remake plan
- [Pavo AI](https://pavoai.app/) — text-to-video and image-to-video with a consistent cast
- [Wan 3.0](https://wan-3.io/) — prompt-first video generation for short-form work
- [H3 Video](https://h3video.org/) — quick cinematic text-to-video clips in the browser
- [Seedance 2.5](https://seedance25.kr/) — smooth motion from text or a single image
- [Viddi AI](https://viddiai.com/) — fast image-to-video for social cuts
- [Kling 4](https://kling4.co.kr/) — Korean walkthroughs and prompt examples for Kling
- [GTA 6 AI Video](https://gta6aivideo.art/) — game-look trailer clips from prompts
- [Grok AI Image Generator](https://grokaiimagegenerator.net/) — free image generation, no signup
- [Reverse Video Search](https://reversevideosearch.org/) — find a clip's source and similar scenes
- [Memories Song](https://memoriessong.com/) — turn a story into a custom song

Occasional write-ups on shot structure, continuity, and what generated footage costs to redo.
Benchmarks live at [ai-video-benchmarks](https://barasel200-bit.github.io/ai-video-benchmarks/).
